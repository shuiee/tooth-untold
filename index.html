What can a tooth remember?

Teeth outlast almost everything else in a grave. This story follows two composite teeth, built from thousands of real ones, through sixteen centuries of European history.

Plate II How far back each record reaches. A line's length is the time its record covers, on one scale for all five, and a gap is a stretch with no record. Circles mark the records within each line; a name opens its section.
